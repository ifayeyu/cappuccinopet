Why we built this

Leo Cappuccino was a bundle of energy and the gentlest, sweetest soul I've ever known. He greeted every human like an old friend, trusted most dogs, ran full-tilt at life. We did what most people do — took him to the vet once a year, watched him grow up, celebrated his birthdays, told ourselves he'd be with us forever. He was healthy. He was happy. He was ours.

Then, in the space of a single visit, that whole life came apart. Osteosarcoma. An aggressive, unforgiving bone cancer that gave us months where we thought we had years. We dove in — read every paper, chased every treatment, called every specialist at every hour we could reach one. We were racing a clock that had almost already run out, and we knew it. Leo turned eight. A few weeks later, he was gone. He took his last breath in my arms.

Cappuccino exists because of him — this platform carries his full name, Leo Cappuccino. Between the annual vet visit and the emergency room visit lives a long, quiet middle — where small changes accumulate, where numbers shift a little each month, where the difference between catching something early and catching it too late is often decided in silence. Leo deserved someone paying attention in that middle. Every dog does. I built Cappuccino so more dogs can live longer, happier lives — because that is what a soul as gentle as his would have wanted. It's what I wanted, too. And now I want it for your dog.

How it works

Three steps.
Then Cappuccino does the rest.

1

Bring the records home.

Drag in lab reports, vaccine records, vet summaries — PDFs, photos, phone scans. Everything lives in one place, per dog, forever.

2

We read. We remember.

Every report gets read, every value gets pulled out and tracked over time — quietly, in the background, for as long as you're a Cappuccino member.

3

A warm briefing every month.

On the first, you get a plain-English report: what's new, what changed, what's worth asking about. Honest, specific, never alarming.

What Cappuccino watches

The quiet things that matter most.

Not just what your vet flagged this visit — the slow-moving numbers between visits that shape a dog's next few years.

Every lab value, every time.

CBC, chemistry, urinalysis, fecal, thyroid, SDMA — all read, normalized, and tracked across every panel your dog has ever had.

Month-over-month deltas.

So you see the creep as it happens — ALT climbing 8% over three months, laid out clearly, month by month.

Every value, even the quiet ones.

Albumin, B12, iron, magnesium — tracked every time, even when nothing's flagged. Nothing gets skipped just because it's technically in range.

Vaccine & screening cadence.

What's due, what's overdue, what your breed needs. Built to the AAHA canine guidelines and updated as they change.

Where we draw the line

What Cappuccino is not.

We believe in being direct about this.

  • 1 Cappuccino is not a diagnosis. We surface patterns; your vet makes calls.
  • 2 Cappuccino is not a replacement for your vet. We make you a better partner in that room.
  • 3 Cappuccino is not an alarm system. We're a calm daily ritual, not a notification firehose.
