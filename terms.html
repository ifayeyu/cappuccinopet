2. What Cappuccino is

4. AI-generated content

Cappuccino uses artificial intelligence (including Anthropic's Claude models) to read uploaded medical records and generate reports. AI systems can make mistakes — they may misread a value, miss context a human would catch, or phrase something imprecisely. Always review AI-extracted values against your original documents, and treat every report as a starting point for a conversation with your vet, not a final word.
