About Cappuccino

A quiet companion, built out of love for one very good dog.

Leo Cappuccino was a bundle of energy and the gentlest, sweetest soul I've ever known. He greeted every human like an old friend, trusted most dogs, ran full-tilt at life. We did what most people do — took him to the vet once a year, watched him grow up, celebrated his birthdays, told ourselves he'd be with us forever. He was healthy. He was happy. He was ours.

Then, in the space of a single visit, that whole life came apart. Osteosarcoma. An aggressive, unforgiving bone cancer that gave us months where we thought we had years. We dove in — read every paper, chased every treatment, called every specialist at every hour we could reach one. We were racing a clock that had almost already run out, and we knew it. Leo turned eight. A few weeks later, he was gone. He took his last breath in my arms.

Cappuccino exists because of him — this platform carries his full name, Leo Cappuccino, so a piece of him is in it every time you open it. Between the annual vet visit and the emergency room visit lives a long, quiet middle — where small changes accumulate, where numbers shift a little each month, where the difference between catching something early and catching it too late is often decided in silence. Leo deserved someone paying attention in that middle. Every dog does. I built Cappuccino so more dogs can live longer, happier lives — because that is what a soul as gentle as his would have wanted. It's what I wanted, too. And now I want it for your dog.

Leo Cappuccino · Feb 23, 2018 – Mar 7, 2026
How we work

The quiet work happens in the background.

Every lab report you upload gets read, every value gets tracked, every trend gets watched — quietly, month after month. We never diagnose and we never alarm. We notice, and then we tell you, plainly and warmly, what's worth a closer look. Think of it less like software and more like a friend who happens to remember every number your vet has ever recorded — and cares enough to keep watching.

What we believe

A few things we won't compromise on.

Your vet leads.

Cappuccino surfaces patterns and asks better questions. It never diagnoses, and it never tells you to skip a vet visit. It makes the visits you do have more useful.

Calm over alarming.

We don't send push notifications designed to spike your anxiety. A monthly briefing, written plainly, is the whole rhythm. No red banners, no urgency theater.

Your data stays yours.

We don't sell pet health data. Ever. Full stop. Read the specifics on our Privacy Policy.
