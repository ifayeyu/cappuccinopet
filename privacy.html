1. Overview

Cappuccino ("we," "us," "our") operates cappuccino.pet and, once launched, a web application that helps dog owners track their pet's health records. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Cappuccino is currently in private, pre-launch development — if you're reading this before the app has publicly launched, some sections describe what will be true once it does, and we've labeled those clearly.

2. What we collect today

Right now, cappuccino.pet is a landing page in private build. The only information we collect is:

  • Email address — if you join our early access list, we store the email you provide.
  • Basic site analytics — aggregate, privacy-first analytics (page views, load performance) that do not use cookies or track you across other websites.

We do not currently collect any information about you or your pet beyond your email address. We don't ask for your name, your dog's name, or anything else at this stage.

Our early access list is handled by Formspree, a third-party form service. When you submit your email, Formspree processes and stores it on our behalf, and may also record technical details such as your IP address and the time of submission as part of its standard spam protection. We use this only to operate the early list. You can ask us to delete your email at any time — see Section 12.

3. What we'll collect once the app launches

When Cappuccino's full product launches, creating an account and using the service will involve collecting:

  • Account information: your name and email address.
  • Pet profile information: your dog's name, breed, sex, birthdate, neuter status, weight, and any diagnoses or medications you choose to record.
  • Uploaded medical records: lab reports, vaccine records, vet visit summaries, and other documents you upload, along with any data our AI extracts from them (test names, values, dates, reference ranges).
  • Usage information: how you interact with the app, to help us fix bugs and improve the product.

You control what you upload. Cappuccino only processes the records you choose to provide.

4. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Send you updates about Cappuccino if you've joined the early list (and only about Cappuccino — never third-party offers).
  • Operate your account and store your pet's records securely.
  • Generate the monthly and yearly health briefings the product is built around, using AI to read and summarize your dog's records.
  • Improve extraction accuracy and product quality — for example, when you correct a value our AI misread, that correction helps us do better next time.
  • Respond to your support requests.

5. AI processing and third parties

Cappuccino uses Anthropic's Claude API to read and extract structured data from the medical records you upload. This means your uploaded documents are transmitted to Anthropic for processing, subject to Anthropic's own data handling terms. We choose AI providers with strong data-handling commitments, and we do not permit training of third-party models on your identifiable pet health data.

We also use infrastructure providers to run the app and store your data securely — currently Supabase (database and file storage). These providers process data on our behalf, under agreements that restrict them from using it for their own purposes.

6. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or your pet's health data to anyone, for any purpose — including advertising. If this ever changes, we will update this policy and ask for your explicit consent first, not bury it in fine print.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, so your dog's full health history stays available to you. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data and uploaded records within 30 days, except where we're required to retain limited information for legal or security purposes. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us (see Section 12).

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and row-level security on our database — to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take this seriously given the sensitivity of health-adjacent data.

9. Children's privacy

Cappuccino is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. We honor these requests regardless of your location. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page as the product evolves, and we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top when we do. For material changes — anything that affects how your data is used — we'll make a reasonable effort to notify you directly, not just post a silent update.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy, or want to exercise a data right? Reach us at hello@cappuccino.pet.
